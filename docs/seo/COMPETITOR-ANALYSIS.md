# Competitor Analysis — expatsdanang.com

*Prepared 2026-09-03. Based on live SERP research plus the technical audit of 2026-09-02.*

## Data limitations — read first

No paid data sources were available for this analysis. Specifically:

- **No DataForSEO** — no keyword volumes, keyword difficulty, traffic estimates, or SERP position tracking.
- **No Moz / Ahrefs** — no domain authority figures for any competitor below. Authority is described qualitatively from observable signals only.
- **No Google Search Console or GA4** — no baseline for our own traffic, rankings, or impressions.
- **Web search is US-geolocated** — results reflect what a searcher *outside* Vietnam sees. That happens to match our dominant audience segment (pre-arrival), but it does **not** represent what someone already in Da Nang sees.

Every competitive claim here is grounded in SERPs actually observed. Where a number would require a tool we don't have, it is absent rather than estimated.

## The market splits into three groups, and nobody occupies the middle

### Group 1 — High-authority data aggregators
`numbeo.com` · `expatistan.com` · `nomads.com`

They own cost-of-living queries outright. Their advantage is structural: crowd-sourced datasets, enormous domain authority, and per-city pages generated at scale. They have no local presence and no services.

**Implication:** `cost-of-living-da-nang` is our thinnest guide (649 words) *and* our most contested SERP. Competing head-on is the worst use of effort in the entire content set.

### Group 2 — Established expat publishers
`expatden.com` · `internationalliving.com` · `vinpearl.com` / `vinwonders.com` · `basedanang.com` · `ahoyvietnam.com` · `mvpvietnam.com` · `xmetr.com`

Deep, well-maintained guide content. `vinpearl`/`vinwonders` carry major Vietnamese brand authority and rank for both informational *and* semi-commercial queries (they rank for "Da Nang motorbike rental"). `basedanang.com` is the closest pure-content analogue to us. They monetise via ads, affiliates, or brand — not service delivery.

**Implication:** we will not out-publish them on general guides. We can beat them on specificity and first-party data.

### Group 3 — Local service operators
Motorbike: `danangbikes.com` · `motorbikerentaldanang.com` · `motorvina.com` · Tuan Motorbike (ranking via TripAdvisor)
Visa: David Tran Consulting · Tân Văn Lang · Phong & Partners · An Visa & Legal
Housing: `rentdanang.app` · Airbnb monthly · Facebook groups

Real businesses with real customers and weak web presence. **David Tran Consulting's top-ranking asset is a Facebook page.** Tân Văn Lang and Phong & Partners rank with law-firm sites carrying little expat-focused content. `mytour.vn` ranks a listicle above most actual providers.

**Implication:** these SERPs are winnable with competent content and technical execution. This is where the opportunity is.

## The strategic gap

| | Trustworthy content | Actual service delivery | Verified inventory |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aggregators | Data only | No | No |
| Publishers | Yes | No | No |
| Local operators | No | Yes | Partial |
| **expatsdanang.com** | **Partial** | **Yes** | **Yes (unpopulated)** |

We are the only player positioned to hold all three. That is the entire strategic thesis: publishers can't fulfil, operators can't publish, aggregators can't do either.

## Direct competitors worth monitoring

**`rentdanang.app`** — the closest competitor to our actual model. Rental inventory plus a content blog ("Living in Da Nang 2026"). They are executing the same combination we are. Watch them.

**`basedanang.com`** — positions as *the* Da Nang expat/nomad guide. Direct competition for our entire `/guides/` cluster.

**`motorvina.com`** — explicitly targets "motorbike rental for expats, long-term options", i.e. exactly our monthly-rental keyword cluster, with English-speaking staff and transparent contracts as stated differentiators. They already occupy the position our three overlapping motorbike guides are fighting each other for.

## Our defensible advantage

Competitors cannot copy **first-party transaction data**. Aggregators publish crowd-sourced averages; publishers publish research; operators publish nothing. We can publish what clients *actually paid* — real rents by neighbourhood, real visa agent fees, real motorbike monthly rates — sourced from our own bookings.

This is simultaneously:
- the hardest thing for competitors to replicate,
- the most citable format for LLMs (specific, attributable, dated),
- and a natural byproduct of operating the business.

No competitor observed in any SERP is doing this.

## Content gaps found in the SERPs

Queries with visible demand and no strong dedicated result from anyone:

- Visa-run legality changes — search results repeatedly flag that the 90-day border-run model is "legally shakier in 2026". Nobody owns an authoritative, current page on this.
- Deposit and passport-holding practices for motorbike rental — surfaced as a recurring expat concern, covered only in scattered forum posts.
- The 3-months-upfront rent convention — mentioned in guides as an arrival-cost shock, no dedicated page anywhere.
- Arrival-buffer budgeting ($3,000–4,000 beyond flight and visa) — no page owns this.

Each maps to a service we sell.
