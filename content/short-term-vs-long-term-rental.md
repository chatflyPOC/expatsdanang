# Short-Term vs Long-Term Apartment Rental: Cost Analysis & When to Choose Each

**Published:** September 2026 | **Read time:** 5 minutes

Unsure whether to book short-term (Airbnb) or commit to a long-term lease? This guide compares costs and helps you choose.

## Cost Comparison: 6-Month Stay

| Metric | Short-term (Airbnb) | Long-term (Lease) | Savings (Long-term) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly rent** | $400–500 | $250–350 | $150–150/month |
| **6-month total** | $2,400–3,000 | $1,500–2,100 | $900–1,500 |
| **Deposit** | $0 (credit card auth) | $300–500 | Refunded after |
| **Setup costs** | $0 (furnished) | $300–500 (furniture) | One-time |
| **Utilities included?** | Usually yes | Separate; varies |  |
| **Cancellation fee** | None | 1-month penalty if early |  |
| **Total real cost** | $2,400–3,000 | $1,800–2,600 | $400–1,200 savings |

**Bottom line:** Long-term is 30–50% cheaper. But you're locked in for 6 months.

## When to Choose Short-Term (Airbnb, Co-living)

### Reasons to Stay Short-Term

| Reason | Duration | Cost Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Testing the city (first visit) | 1–2 months | Pay premium ($400+/month) for flexibility |
| Unsure how long you'll stay | 1–3 months | Not worth long-term commitment yet |
| Visiting for work contract (uncertain end date) | 1–6 months | Premium worth the exit flexibility |
| Waiting for longer-term apartment to become available | 1–2 months | Temporary solution while searching |
| Need furnished + move-in immediately | Any duration | Can't wait 2–3 weeks for furnished rental |
| Want community (co-living) | 3–6 months | Pay $50–100/month extra for built-in social life |

**Verdict:** If unsure about the city, staying <3 months, or value flexibility over cost—go short-term.

---

## When to Choose Long-Term (6-Month+ Lease)

### Reasons to Commit

| Reason | Lease Duration | Savings |
|---|---|---|
| Staying 6+ months (know you like the city) | 6–12 months | Save $100–150/month ($600–1,800 total) |
| Work contract is stable (job security) | 12 months | Maximum savings; most commitments |
| Starting to build community (want stability) | 12 months | Worth locking in; nest-building phase |
| Relocated with family | 12 months+ | Security; kids need stable address |
| Remote worker (indefinite stay possible) | 12 months | Lock in rate; renew if happy |

**Verdict:** If staying 6+ months, committed to the city, want to save money—go long-term.

## The "Try First" Strategy (Recommended)

### Phase 1: Short-term Airbnb or Co-living (1 month)

**Cost:** $400–500
**Goal:** Test neighborhoods, meet people, decide if staying

**Advantage:** No commitment; can leave anytime

### Phase 2: Month-to-Month Direct Lease (1–2 months)

**Cost:** $300–350/month (negotiate lower than Airbnb; higher than 6-month)
**Goal:** Lock in a neighborhood; build community; delay long-term commitment

**Advantage:** Lower cost than Airbnb; still flexible (30-day exit)

### Phase 3: Long-Term Lease (6–12 months)

**Cost:** $250–300/month (locked in once you decide)
**Goal:** Settle in; maximize cost savings; build stability

**Advantage:** Cheapest option; most control over space; community investment

**Total 3-month transition: ~$1,300**
vs
**Direct 6-month lease: ~$1,700 (same duration, less flexibility)**

**Difference:** $400 for 3 months of flexibility; often worth it.

## Seasonal Pricing: When to Commit

### Peak Season (Oct–Nov) — Higher Prices

| Rental Type | Cost | Negotiability |
|---|---|---|
| Airbnb | $500–600/month | Low (high demand) |
| Month-to-month | $350–400/month | Low |
| 6-month lease | $300–350/month | Low |

**Strategy:** Book 6-month lease in Oct–Nov if committed (lock in mid-range pricing).

### Shoulder Season (Dec–Feb) — Better Prices

| Rental Type | Cost | Negotiability |
|---|---|---|
| Airbnb | $350–450/month | Medium |
| Month-to-month | $280–320/month | Medium (easier to negotiate) |
| 6-month lease | $250–300/month | Medium |

**Strategy:** Test Airbnb in Dec–Jan (cheaper). If you like it, negotiate long-term starting Feb (leverage: "I'll commit 6 months if you lock in current rate").

### Off-Season (Mar–Aug) — Cheapest Prices

| Rental Type | Cost | Negotiability |
|---|---|---|
| Airbnb | $300–400/month | High (low demand) |
| Month-to-month | $200–250/month | High |
| 6-month lease | $180–250/month | High |

**Strategy:** Best time to negotiate. If staying this period, lock in long-term (won't find cheaper).

## Early Termination: Cost of Changing Your Mind

### Short-Term (Airbnb)

**Change your mind after 1 week?**
- Cancellation policy varies (strict, moderate, flexible)
- Strict: Lose 50% of payment; can cancel 1 week before
- Moderate: Lose 50% of 1 night; cancel 5 days before
- Flexible: Full refund if cancel 1 day before

**Reality:** You can leave early, but forfeit payment. Expect $100–200 loss.

### Month-to-Month Direct Lease

**Change your mind after 2 weeks?**
- Standard lease: Requires 30-day notice
- If you leave: Pay 30-day penalty (full month's rent)
- Cost: $250–350 to exit

**Reality:** You can leave, but pay a month's rent (expensive).

### 6-Month Lease

**Change your mind after 2 months?**
- Lease: Requires you to stay 6 months OR pay early termination fee
- Typical fee: 1–2 months' rent
- Cost: $250–500 to exit early

**Reality:** Exiting costs; landlord is unhappy; recommend negotiating instead.

## Decision Tree

```
1. How long will you stay in Da Nang?
   ├─ 1–4 weeks → Airbnb/guesthouse (short-term optimal)
   ├─ 1–3 months → Airbnb or 1-month Airbnb (test before committing)
   └─ 3+ months → Long-term lease (save money)

2. How sure are you about staying?
   ├─ Very unsure → Short-term (flexibility > cost)
   ├─ Somewhat sure → Month-to-month (balance)
   └─ Very sure → Long-term (savings > flexibility)

3. Current season?
   ├─ Oct–Nov → Long-term lease (mid pricing; good deals available)
   ├─ Dec–Feb → Test Airbnb; then negotiate long-term
   └─ Mar–Aug → Lock in long-term (cheapest prices)
```

## Negotiation: Upgrading Short-term to Long-term

**Scenario:** You've been in Airbnb 1 month; love the apartment + landlord

**Your pitch:**
- "I've loved this place. I want to stay 6 months long-term. Can you give me a 6-month rate instead of Airbnb monthly?"
- Landlord: "How much lower than your current $450/month?"
- You: "I could do $300/month for 6 months (you save Airbnb fees; I'm committed long-term)"
- Landlord: Usually accepts ($1,800 guaranteed 6-month revenue vs $450 monthly uncertainty)

**Outcome:** $300/month lease (saves $150/month vs Airbnb)

## FAQ

**Q: Is 6-month lease worth it if I might leave after 3 months?**
A: Only if you're 70%+ sure you're staying. If <50% sure, Airbnb/month-to-month is worth the premium.

**Q: Can I negotiate out of a lease early?**
A: Sometimes. Offer to find replacement tenant; pays relocation costs. Expect to pay 1 month's penalty.

**Q: Is it cheaper to Airbnb year-round vs lease?**
A: No. Lease is always cheaper long-term (30–50% savings over 12 months).

**Q: When is the best time to sign a long-term lease?**
A: Mar–Aug (lowest prices, landlords hungry). Oct–Nov is okay (mid pricing). Avoid Dec–Feb peak.

**Q: Can I break a lease if I get a job offer elsewhere?**
A: Most leases allow early termination with 30–60 day notice + 1 month penalty. Negotiate before signing.
