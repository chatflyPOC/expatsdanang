# 10 Common Big Bike Rental Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

**Published:** September 2026 | **Read time:** 4 minutes

Avoid costly errors, damage disputes, and safety issues.

## Mistake 1: Renting Without Reading the Contract
**Error:** Signing without English translation; missing damage clauses.
**Fix:** Request English version; ask rental to **explain** each section verbally.

## Mistake 2: Skipping the Pre-Ride Walkthrough
**Error:** Not inspecting bike for pre-existing damage; rental later claims you caused scratches.
**Fix:** Take photos of every angle (wide + close-ups); write down odometer reading.

## Mistake 3: Running Out of Gas on Mountain Pass
**Error:** Assuming gas station exists; running out in remote area.
**Fix:** Fill before Hai Van Pass / Ho Chi Minh Trail sections; carry reserve jerry can on multi-day trips.

## Mistake 4: No Insurance (or Wrong Insurance)
**Error:** "I'm a careful rider; I don't need insurance." One mistake = $5,000 bill.
**Fix:** Mandatory comprehensive coverage ($15-25/day); worth every dollar.

## Mistake 5: Riding Exhausted or Hungover
**Error:** Mountain passes demand full focus; fatigue kills.
**Fix:** Sleep 8 hours before Hai Van; avoid alcohol 12hrs before riding.

## Mistake 6: Trusting Weather Forecasts (Monsoon)
**Error:** "Forecast says clear; I'll leave at noon." Monsoon afternoon storms hit fast.
**Fix:** Ride early (6-11am); descend passes before 2pm June-Aug.

## Mistake 7: Cheap Helmet or Wrong Gear
**Error:** Renting a helmet that doesn't fit; wearing t-shirt on mountain road.
**Fix:** Bring your own DOT-certified helmet; wear jacket + gloves minimum.

## Mistake 8: Late Return Penalty
**Error:** "Traffic delayed me; I'll return 1 hour late." Penalty = 1M VND/hour (~$45).
**Fix:** Set phone alarm 30min before deadline; plan route with 30min buffer.

## Mistake 9: Riding Unfamiliar Bike Too Far Too Fast
**Error:** Pick up bike → ride straight to Hue without test ride. Unfamiliar brakes/throttle = crashes.
**Fix:** 15min local test ride; practice brake modulation before committing to pass.

## Mistake 10: Not Downloading Maps Offline
**Error:** Lost 4G signal on mountain road; no GPS; wandering 2 hours in wrong direction.
**Fix:** Download Locus Maps or Gaia GPS (offline) the day before.
