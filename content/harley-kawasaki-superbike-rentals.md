### Why Rent Heavyweight Bikes in Vietnam?

1. **Experience iconic brands** you may not own at home
2. **Learn on premium machines** with better brakes, suspension, ergonomics
3. **Photograph heritage** on legendary bikes (Harleys photograph beautifully in Vietnam)
4. **Road test before buying** (many riders use rentals as extended test drives)

### Harley-Davidson Street 750 vs Street Glide

**Street 750:** Entry-level Harley; smaller engine; lighter (215 kg)
- Better for tight mountain passes
- Lower seat (690mm); easier for shorter riders
- Lower daily cost
- Still iconic sound and feel

**Street Glide:** Full-size cruiser; powerful 1000cc+; heavier (365 kg)
- Better for long highways
- Massive comfort on 8+ hour days
- Premium saddlebags; highway pegs
- Premium rental cost

**Verdict:** Street 750 for Hai Van Pass; Street Glide for Da Nang → Saigon highway runs.

## Superbike Reality Check

Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R and Ducati Panigale rentals exist but are **rarely booked by tourists** because:

1. **High deposit:** $1,000–2,000+ liability hold
2. **Insurance expensive:** Often not included; add $30–50/day
3. **Skill requirement:** These bikes have 200+ horsepower; they demand respect
4. **Limited roads:** Hai Van Pass is fun on a superbike but technically overkill (and dangerous if you're not experienced)
5. **Fuel hungry:** 30-liter tanks drain fast at high throttle

**Our recommendation:** Rent a superbike only if you:
- Own a powerful bike at home
- Have track day experience
- Are on a long-distance highway trip (Saigon → Hanoi)
- Want the experience/photos

For Hai Van Pass? Stick with 300–650cc.

## The Harley Experience in Vietnam

Riding a Harley in Vietnam is **very different** from riding in the USA:

**Challenges:**
- Vietnamese roads are narrower; Harleys are wide
- Mountain passes have tight switchbacks; Harley weight (250+ kg) punishes sloppy cornering
- Fuel consumption higher; 300km range on a 21L tank
- Other riders (mostly on 100cc scooters) treat you like a celebrity

**Rewards:**
- **Iconic sound** reverberates through Da Nang valleys
- **Photo ops** on Harleys in Vietnam are legendary
- **Cultural moment** for some riders; bucket list experience
- **Smooth cruising** on highways (Harleys love flat roads)

**Best Harley route in Da Nang:** Flat coastal run (Da Nang → Hoi An → Quang Ngai), not mountain passes.

## Rental Requirements for Heavy Bikes

Rental companies enforce stricter checks for 650cc+ bikes:

1. **Driving license proof:** Must show home country license + IDP (not negotiable)
2. **Age requirement:** Minimum 25 years old for 1000cc+ bikes
3. **Riding experience:** Usually require proof (riding resume or prior rental history)
4. **Deposit:** $500–2,000 USD depending on bike value
5. **Insurance:** Mandatory; often $30–50/day extra

**Pro tip:** If you're a first-timer on big bikes, start with a 500cc–650cc, then upgrade. Rental companies track rental history and trust experienced riders more.

## FAQ: Superbike & Harley Rentals

**Q: Can a new rider rent a Harley Street Glide?**
A: Yes, if licensed and 18+. However, it's heavy (365 kg) and slow-speed instability may scare inexperienced riders. Try a Street 750 first.

**Q: What's the damage if I crash a Harley?**
A: Expect $2,000–5,000+ repair costs. Your deposit covers first $500–1,000; you pay the rest. Always get insurance.

**Q: Is a superbike worth renting in Da Nang?**
A: Only if you have track experience or want the thrill factor. For sightseeing, a 300–650cc is more practical.

**Q: Can I take a rented Harley on Hai Van Pass?**
A: Technically yes, but not recommended. It's slow in switchbacks, hard to maneuver, and consumes fuel fast climbing. Save Harleys for highways.
