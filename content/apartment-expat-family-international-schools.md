# Family Housing for Expats in Da Nang: Finding Apartments Near International Schools

**Published:** September 2026 | **Read time:** 5 minutes

Families relocating need housing within school commute range + expat-friendly amenities.

## International Schools in Da Nang

| School | Location | Commute from My Khe | Grades | Fees |
|--------|----------|------------------|-------|------|
| Abc International School | An Phu | 10 min | K-5 | 200M+ VND/year |
| Da Nang International School (DNIS) | Hai Chau | 15 min | K-12 | 180M+ VND/year |

## Best Family Neighborhoods

**Closest to schools:** An Phu, Ngu Hanh Son (Abc school), Hai Chau (DNIS)
**Safest:** My Khe, An Thuong (beach-adjacent; international expats)
**Cheapest:** Hai Chau, Cam Le (local area; 20-25min to school)

## Family Housing Checklist

- ✅ 2+ bedrooms (kids need own rooms)
- ✅ Kitchen (cook Western food if needed)
- ✅ Balcony/garden (kids play safely)
- ✅ Apartment complex (security, other expat families)
- ✅ Maid service available (2-3x/week, 800k-1.2M VND)
