## Best Neighborhoods for Furnished Rentals

**Short-term friendly (1-6 months):** My Khe, An Thuong, Ngu Hanh Son
**Long-term (6+ months):** Hai Chau, Cam Le (cheaper, fewer tourists)

## Rental Platforms

- **Airbnb:** Highest prices; most furnished; shorter terms (weeks-months)
- **Booking.com:** Medium price; monthly discounts available
- **Local Facebook groups:** "Da Nang Apartments," "Da Nang Rentals" — cheapest, direct landlord
- **Direct (word-of-mouth):** Lowest price if you find locals

## Deposit & Contract

**Typical terms:**
- 1-month advance payment + 1-month deposit (refundable)
- 3-month minimum (negotiable)
- Utilities: 500k–1.5M VND extra/month (electricity + water)
