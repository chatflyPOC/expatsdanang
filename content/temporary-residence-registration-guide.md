# Temporary Residence Registration (TRR) Guide: Vietnamese Police Filing for Expats

**Published:** September 2026 | **Read time:** 5 minutes

Vietnam's Temporary Residence Registration (TRR) is a mandatory police filing for foreigners staying 24+ hours in one location. This guide covers the 24-hour deadline, paperwork, costs, and common pitfalls.

## TRR Basics: What You Must Know

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Who needs it?** | Every foreigner staying 24+ hours (tourists, expats, volunteers) |
| **Deadline** | Within 24 hours of arrival at accommodation |
| **Who files it?** | Hotel/guesthouse (automatic) OR landlord/expat (if staying in private apartment) |
| **Cost** | Free to foreigners; hotel/landlord pays $0.50–$2 admin fee |
| **Duration** | Covers the entire stay at that address |
| **Penalty for non-filing** | Fine: 100,000–300,000 VND (~$4–$12) if caught; rarely enforced for tourists |

## How TRR Works in Practice

### Scenario 1: Hotel/Guesthouse Stay

**Good news:** Hotel handles TRR automatically.

**What happens:**
1. You check in (provide passport)
2. Hotel staff takes passport info + photocopy
3. Hotel files TRR with local police (usually same day)
4. You get receipt (they keep it or email it)

**Your responsibility:** None. Hotel does everything.

**Cost:** Included in hotel fee (or $1–2 extra; varies).

### Scenario 2: Private Apartment Rental (Long-term)

**Problem:** Landlord may not know about TRR or may ignore it.

**What should happen:**
1. You move in
2. Within 24 hours, landlord (or you) must file TRR with local police station
3. Police record your address + departure date
4. You get registration certificate (keep for visa extensions)

**Landlord responsibility:** Most experienced landlords know this. New/inexperienced landlords may not.

**Your action:** Ask landlord on move-in day: "Will you file TRR for me?" If unsure, help them file or file yourself.

## The TRR Filing Process (DIY)

### Required Documents

- Passport (original + photocopy)
- Apartment rental contract (photocopy)
- Completed TRR form (police provide blank copy)
- Proof of accommodation (lease or police already have it)

### Step-by-Step Filing (DIY)

**Step 1: Locate your local police station** (Công an)
- Ask landlord or Google "Công an [your district] Da Nang"
- Most neighborhoods have one within walking distance

**Step 2: Arrive with documents**
- Bring passport + copies + lease copy
- Go during business hours (8 AM–11 AM, 1 PM–4 PM)
- No appointment needed

**Step 3: Complete form**
- Police provide blank TRR form (in Vietnamese)
- Fill in: Name, passport #, address, expected departure date
- Landlord may need to sign/stamp (check with police)

**Step 4: Pay + receive receipt**
- Foreigner pays nothing (if required, ~50,000 VND/$2)
- Police process immediately
- You receive certificate/receipt (keep for records)

**Time needed:** 15–30 minutes total

## TRR & Visa Extensions: Critical Connection

### Why TRR Matters for Visa

| Situation | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| Extending 3-month tourist visa | TRR proves legal address (immigration needs this) |
| Applying for work permit | TRR proves 24+ hour residence (work permit requirement) |
| Switching from tourist to business visa | TRR required; must be current |
| Leaving Vietnam + returning | TRR for each new address (if multi-month trip in and out) |

**Example:**
- You arrive in Da Nang on tourist visa (30 days)
- Hotel files TRR automatically ✓
- Day 15: You move to apartment
- Day 16: Landlord must file new TRR (previous one no longer valid)
- Day 25: You apply for 30-day extension
- Immigration reviews TRR; if current, extension is approved

**If you didn't file TRR:** Immigration might question your address; extension could be denied or delayed.

## TRR Renewal & Address Changes

### Changing Addresses (Mid-Lease Move)

**Scenario:** You're in apartment for 3 months. After 1.5 months, you move to a different district.

**What to do:**
1. File TRR for new address (within 24 hours)
2. Keep old TRR (you have two now; both valid for different periods)
3. New landlord files new TRR with local police

**Police know you moved** (TRR is updated in their system).

### TRR Duration

- **Expires when?** When you leave that address (you leave apartment = TRR ends)
- **Do you need to "cancel" it?** No; police assume you left
- **If you return to same address later?** New TRR filing required (treated as new arrival)

## Common TRR Mistakes

### Mistake #1: Forgetting to File for Apartment

**Problem:** Move into apartment; assume TRR is automatic (like hotel). It's not.

**Reality:** Police don't know you're there unless landlord files.

**Consequence:** If caught during police check (rare for tourists; common for expats), fine of 100k–300k VND.

**Prevention:** Ask landlord on Day 1: "Will you file TRR?" If no, file it yourself.

### Mistake #2: Not Updating TRR When Moving

**Problem:** Moved to new apartment; old TRR still active.

**Reality:** You now have mismatched TRR (old address) vs actual address.

**Consequence:** Visa extension complications; police confusion if checked.

**Prevention:** File new TRR every time you change addresses.

### Mistake #3: Losing Your TRR Receipt

**Problem:** Didn't keep copy of TRR receipt; can't prove filing.

**Reality:** Police have records (they filed it). You can request copy.

**Prevention:** Take photo of TRR receipt; email to yourself. Always keep originals for 3+ months.

## TRR Enforcement: How Likely Are You to Be Checked?

### Who Gets Checked?

- **Tourists:** Rarely (police focus on expats)
- **Expats on tourist visa:** Occasionally (immigration enforcement increasing)
- **Long-term residents:** Frequently (apartment blocks are registered)

### What Happens if Caught Without TRR

**Scenario:** Police stop you for random check (very rare in Da Nang)

**If no TRR filed:**
- Fine: 100,000–300,000 VND (~$4–$12)
- Police may escort you to file (same day)
- Rare; but possible

**Likelihood:** <1% for short-term tourists; 5–10% for 6+ month expats

**Prevention:** Just file it. Takes 30 minutes; costs nothing; eliminates all risk.

## TRR FAQ

**Q: Do tourists really need TRR?**
A: Yes, but hotels file it automatically. You don't think about it.

**Q: What if my landlord refuses to file TRR?**
A: File it yourself at police station (takes 30 min; they can't refuse you).

**Q: Does TRR affect my visa status?**
A: It documents where you're living; doesn't change your visa type. But it's required for visa extensions.

**Q: Can I file TRR online?**
A: No; must go in person to police station. Government hasn't digitized this yet.

**Q: What if I move houses during my stay?**
A: File new TRR at new address (old one still valid but you live at new address now).

**Q: Do I need TRR for Airbnb stays?**
A: Theoretically yes, but Airbnb hosts file it. Enforcement is inconsistent.
