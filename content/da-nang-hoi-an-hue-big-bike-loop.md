# 3-Day Da Nang–Hoi An–Hue Big Bike Loop: Complete Itinerary for Riders

**Published:** September 2026 | **Read time:** 7 minutes | **Distance:** 225 km

## The Perfect 3-Day Big Bike Adventure

The Da Nang–Hoi An–Hue loop is Southeast Asia's most iconic motorcycle journey: 225 km combining Hai Van Pass (5-star technical riding), coastal highways, and world heritage sites.

### Day-by-Day Breakdown

**Day 1: Da Nang → Hoi An (30 km, 1 hour)**
- Pickup bike 8am in Da Nang
- Ride flat coastal road south to Hoi An
- 10am arrival; explore Ancient Town (UNESCO site)
- Afternoon: Cua Dai Beach, local lunch
- Evening: Lantern Town night market
- **Overnight:** Hoi An (mid-range hotel ~400k VND, $18)

**Day 2: Hoi An → Hue via Hai Van Pass (105 km, 4 hours)**
- 8am depart Hoi An
- Climb Hai Van Pass (2.5 hours of technical riding)
- 11am photo stop at 470m viewpoint
- Descend to Hue (1 hour)
- 1pm lunch in Hue
- Afternoon: Citadel exploration, Thanh Noi pagoda
- Evening: Perfume River sunset cruise
- **Overnight:** Hue (400–600k VND, $18–27)

**Day 3: Hue → Da Nang via Hai Van Return (105 km, 4 hours)**
- 8am depart Hue
- Climb Hai Van Pass (return; descending on Da Nang side)
- 12pm return to Da Nang; lunch
- 2pm return bike to rental company
- 3pm finish

## Best Time to Ride

- **Sep–Oct:** Ideal (clear, 25°C, light traffic)
- **Nov–Feb:** Good but crowded (tourism peak)
- **Mar–Apr:** Good (warm, 28°C)
- **May–Aug:** Hot, humid, typhoon risk

## Stops & Must-See Attractions

### Hoi An (Day 1)
- **Ancient Town:** UNESCO pedestrian zone; 2,400+ buildings (1–2 hours)
- **Cua Dai Beach:** Quiet; fewer tourists than My Khe
- **Thanh Ha Pottery Village:** Working clay kilns; demonstrations
- **Lantern Town:** Evening market; 7–10pm best

### Hai Van Pass (Day 2)
- **470m Viewpoint:** 15-min photo stop; views to Hue/Danang
- **Bunker Tour:** Optional; French military bunker (30 min detour)

### Hue (Day 2–3)
- **Citadel:** Imperial fortress; 2–3 hours walking
- **Thanh Noi Pagoda:** Riverside temple; serene
- **Perfume River Sunset Cruise:** Evening boat tour; 1–2 hours
- **Royal Tombs:** Minh Mang Tomb, Tu Duc Tomb (outside city)

## Bike Recommendations for This Loop

**Best choice:** Honda CB300R or Versys-X 300
- Light enough for Hai Van Pass
- Comfortable for 4-hour days
- Excellent fuel economy (~25 km/liter)
- Easy to park and maneuver in Hoi An/Hue

**Avoid:** Harley Street Glide (too heavy for passes; fuel-hungry)

---

## Safety & Practical Notes

1. **Fuel up before Hai Van Pass** — no gas stations on pass
2. **Return before dark** — mountain passes unsafe after 6pm
3. **Helmet mandatory** — police checkpoints frequent on Highway 1
4. **Secure your bike** — lock when exploring; never leave valuables
5. **Download maps offline** — spotty 4G in mountain areas

## FAQ: Da Nang–Hoi An–Hue Loop

**Q: Is 3 days too fast?**
A: For riders with experience, yes. Ideal is 4–5 days. But 3 days is doable if you skip detailed sightseeing.

**Q: Can I do this solo?**
A: Absolutely. Solo riders commonly do this loop; no guides needed.

**Q: What if I'm a beginner rider?**
A: Doable but challenging. Hai Van Pass is technical. Consider a 4–5 day loop with an extra rest day to build confidence.

**Q: Best season to go?**
A: Sep–Oct (cool, clear, low humidity); Nov–Feb (crowded but pleasant).
