# Travel Insurance for Motorcycle Rentals in Vietnam: Complete Guide

**Published:** September 2026 | **Read time:** 4 minutes

Your home travel insurance might NOT cover motorcycle accidents. Here's what you need.

## Types of Coverage Needed

| Coverage | What It Does | Cost | Essential? |
|----------|-------------|------|-----------|
| Bike damage | Repairs to rental bike | 50-150k/day | YES |
| Medical evacuation | Helicopter + hospital | 30-50k/day | YES |
| Personal liability | Injury to others | Included | YES |
| Theft | Stolen bike | 40-80k/day | YES if touring |

## Check Your Home Insurance

**Most policies EXCLUDE:**
- Motorcycles over 125cc
- Rental vehicle damage
- Adventure sports

**Call your insurer before traveling** and ask specifically about 300cc+ motorcycle rentals in Vietnam.
