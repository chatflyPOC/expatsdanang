## Insurance: What's Covered?

### Included in Base Rental (Usually)
- Third-party liability (other people's injuries/damage)
- Stolen bike coverage (if properly locked)

### NOT Included (Add-On Options)
- **Bike damage:** 50–100k VND/day adds coverage; reduces your liability from 100% to 20–30%
- **Personal injury:** 30–50k VND/day covers hospital bills
- **Theft/vandalism:** 40–80k VND/day (essential for Hoi An/Hue overnights)

### What Insurance DOESN'T Cover
- Damage from racing or stunts
- Damage while intoxicated
- Damage from negligence (leaving bike unattended in sketchy areas)
- Theft if keys left in bike

## Negotiation Tips

1. **Book 7+ days:** Qualify for 15–25% discount
2. **Rent off-peak:** Mid-week cheaper than weekends
3. **Ask for "damage waiver":** Often negotiable to 10–15% flat fee vs. variable deposit
4. **Bundle insurance:** Ask for discount if you add multi-day insurance
5. **Loyalty:** Return customers often get 10–20% loyalty discounts

**Sample negotiation:**
- *"I'm renting 10 days; what's your best price?"*
- *"I want comprehensive insurance; can you bundle that into one rate?"*
- *"What if I rent two bikes for my friend and me?"*

## Common Insurance Disputes (Avoid These)

| Dispute | Cause | Prevention |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| Pre-existing damage charged to you | Rental company claims you caused it | **Take photos BEFORE pickup** of entire bike; get written confirmation |
| Deposit held indefinitely | Rental company disputes damage extent | Use credit card (easier dispute process); pay with cash only as last resort |
| Insurance claim denied | Insurer claims negligence | Keep all receipts; get written incident report from rental company |
| Late fee surprise | Misunderstanding of return time | Confirm return time IN WRITING; set phone alarm 30 min before |

## FAQ: Pricing & Insurance

**Q: Can I use my own travel insurance for motorcycle rental?**
A: Rare. Most exclude or limit motorcycles. Always get bike-specific insurance from rental company.

**Q: Is theft insurance worth it in Da Nang?**
A: Essential if leaving bike overnight in Hoi An/Hue. Low-cost ($5–10/day) relative to bike value ($5,000–15,000).

**Q: What if I return the bike with a scratch?**
A: Report it immediately. If unreported, rental company may charge for repair. Most minor scratches (< 5cm) are waived if pre-documented.

**Q: Can I negotiate the deposit down?**
A: Sometimes. Offer to provide ID + extra credit card authorization instead. Many companies agree to $200 hold instead of $500.
