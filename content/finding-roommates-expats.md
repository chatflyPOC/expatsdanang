# Finding Roommates in Da Nang: Expat Edition Housing Costs & House Rules

**Published:** September 2026 | **Read time:** 5 minutes

Sharing a house or apartment with roommates cuts rent by 40–50%. This guide covers finding compatible roommates, splitting expenses, and setting house rules.

## Roommate Economics: Can You Save Half?

### 2-Bedroom Apartment Cost Breakdown

| Situation | Rent per person | Monthly savings vs 1BR |
|---|---|---|
| Solo (full 2BR rent) | $400–500 | $0 (pay all) |
| With 1 roommate | $200–250 | $200–250 |
| With 2 roommates | $133–167 | $267–333 |

**Example:** 2-bedroom = $400/month total. Split 2 ways = $200/person (save 50% vs 1BR apartment at $350).

### Shared House (3–4 Bedrooms)

| Configuration | Rent/person | Your room size |
|---|---|---|
| 4-bedroom house ($600/month) | $150 | Small–medium (10m²) |
| 3-bedroom house ($400/month) | $133 | Medium (12–15m²) |

**Reality:** Shared houses are cheapest; trade-off is shared kitchen/living space.

## Vetting Roommates: Questions to Ask

### Before Committing

**Personality/Lifestyle:**
- [ ] What are your work hours? (9–5 office vs flexible remote)
- [ ] Do you cook? (Share kitchen? Eat out?)
- [ ] Noise level? (Music, parties, quiet evenings?)
- [ ] Guests policy? (How often? Overnight guests?)
- [ ] Cleaning standards? (Daily tidying vs weekly)?
- [ ] Smoking/drinking/drugs? (What's acceptable in shared space?)

**Practical:**
- [ ] How long staying? (3 months vs 1 year?)
- [ ] Income/financial stability? (Can you pay rent?)
- [ ] Ever been evicted or had housing disputes?
- [ ] What's your moving-out notice? (30 days? 60 days?)

**Compatibility:**
- [ ] Religion/cultural background? (Any dietary restrictions to respect?)
- [ ] Pets? (Allergies?)
- [ ] Relationship status? (Will partner move in later?)
- [ ] Mental health/drama? (Transparent communication?)

### Red Flags

| Warning Sign | Why It's Bad | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Vague about income/job | May not pay rent on time | Ask for proof of employment/income |
| Won't answer lifestyle questions | Hiding something (messy, party-prone, etc) | Skip; trust your gut |
| Recently evicted or disputes with past roommates | Conflict pattern | Hard pass |
| Wants you to pay full deposit before meeting | Scam risk | Don't send money until meeting in person |
| No references from past roommates | Unverifiable track record | Risky; meet in person first |

## Shared Expense Splitting

### What to Split (Fairly)

| Expense | Split equally? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Rent | Yes | Each person gets equal space |
| Utilities (electricity) | Pro-rata (who uses more) OR equal | Most split equally for simplicity |
| Water | Equal | Small cost; not worth tracking |
| Internet | Equal | Shared connection |
| Household supplies (soap, TP) | Rotate buying OR equal | Either method works |
| Cleaning service (if shared) | Equal | Shared benefit |

**Easiest method:** Split utilities 3 equal ways (even if one person uses AC more; close enough).

### Monthly Spreadsheet Template

```
Roommate Rent Split (3-bedroom $450/month):
- Roommate A (largest room): $180
- Roommate B (medium room): $145
- Roommate C (small room): $125

Utilities ($100 electricity/month): Split equally
- Each pays: $33.33

Total per person:
- A: $213.33
- B: $178.33
- C: $158.33
```

### Payment System

**Best method:** Shared account or Wise transfer

1. Designate one person as "treasurer"
2. All roommates transfer money to treasure on Day 1 of month
3. Treasure pays landlord + utilities (one transaction)
4. Monthly reconciliation (each person's share logged)

**Alternative:** Cash + notebook (informal; works for 2–3 roommates)

## House Rules Agreement (Put in Writing)

### Essential Rules

| Rule | Specific |
|---|---|
| **Rent payment** | Due on [1st of month]. Late fee: $20 (encourages on-time payment). |
| **Quiet hours** | 10 PM–7 AM (no loud music, guests must leave by 11 PM). |
| **Guests policy** | Guests okay on weekends. Overnight guests max 2x per week. Let roommates know in advance. |
| **Cleaning** | Shared spaces cleaned weekly (rotate cleaning schedule). Private room = your responsibility. |
| **Kitchen** | Clean dishes immediately after use. Don't leave food in fridge >1 week. |
| **Laundry** | Washing machine: 1 load per person per week (share fairly). Dryer: Machine shared; line-dry when possible. |
| **Utilities** | AC to 20°C minimum (saves electricity). Lights off when leaving room. |
| **Early exit** | 30-day notice required. If you leave early, pay 1 month rent as penalty. |
| **Conflicts** | Discuss issues calmly. If unresolved, seek third-party mediation (landlord or Da Nang Expats group admin). |

### Dispute Resolution

**Level 1:** Direct conversation ("Hey, I noticed the kitchen is messy..." / "Can we revisit quiet hours?")

**Level 2:** House meeting (all roommates discuss issue; document resolution)

**Level 3:** Mediation (ask landlord or trusted expat friend to mediate)

**Level 4:** Exit (roommate leaves with 30-day notice if incompatible)

## When to Leave: Exit Gracefully

### Reasons to Find New Roommates

- Personality conflict (unresolvable after 1 month)
- Violation of house rules (excessive noise, guests, cleanliness)
- Rent disputes (roommate can't pay; you're covering)
- Life change (new job requires commute; house too far)

### The 30-Day Notice

**Standard:** 30-day notice to landlord + remaining roommates

**Your cost:** 1 additional month rent (unless other roommate replaces you immediately)

**Example:**
- Day 1: Tell roommates "I'm leaving in 30 days"
- Day 30: Move out
- Day 31: Remaining roommates find replacement; you're off the hook

## FAQ

**Q: Is it weird to live with strangers as an adult?**
A: No; very common for expats in Da Nang. Most 25–45 year-olds live with roommates (saves $200/month).

**Q: Can I have overnight guests in shared housing?**
A: Yes, with roommate approval. Most agree to 2–3x per week max.

**Q: What if a roommate stops paying rent?**
A: You're legally liable for full amount (landlord bills you). Evict roommate; find replacement.

**Q: Best neighborhoods for shared apartments?**
A: An Thuong (affordable + social) or Hai Chau (cheapest). Avoid Ngu Hanh Son (expat bubble less suited for roommate hunting).

**Q: How long should I commit to roommate situation?**
A: Start with 3-month trial. If working well, renew lease together. If not, find individual apartments.
