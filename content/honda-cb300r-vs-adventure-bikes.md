# Honda CB300R vs Other 300cc Adventure Bikes: Complete 2026 Comparison

**Published:** September 2026 | **Read time:** 6 minutes

## The 300cc Sweet Spot

300cc is the Goldilocks engine size for big bike rentals: enough power for highways, light enough for technical riding, affordable, and fuel-efficient.

## Head-to-Head: CB300R vs Competitors

### Honda CB300R
- **Engine:** 286cc parallel-twin; 30bhp; 21Nm torque
- **Weight:** 146 kg (lightest)
- **Seat height:** 820mm
- **Tank:** 15.2L (range: 380 km)
- **Pricing:** $11–15/day
- **Best for:** Hai Van Pass (agility), technical mountain riding
- **Strengths:** Light, nimble, affordable, twin-cylinder smoothness
- **Weaknesses:** Upright riding only; minimal fairing/windscreen

### Kawasaki Versys-X 300
- **Engine:** 300cc parallel-twin; 38bhp; 27.1Nm (5% more power)
- **Weight:** 168 kg (22 kg heavier)
- **Seat height:** 860mm
- **Tank:** 17L (range: 420 km)
- **Pricing:** $15–20/day
- **Best for:** Long-distance touring; comfort
- **Strengths:** Adventure fairings, windscreen, taller, more planted
- **Weaknesses:** Heavier; less flickable in switchbacks

### Royal Enfield Himalayan 350
- **Engine:** 350cc single; 29bhp; 32Nm (higher torque)
- **Weight:** 190 kg
- **Seat height:** 800mm (low)
- **Tank:** 15L
- **Pricing:** $12–18/day
- **Best for:** Rough roads, off-road capability
- **Strengths:** Legendary reliability, torquey engine, iconic design
- **Weaknesses:** Heavy vibration (single-cylinder), older suspension

## CB300R vs Versys-X 300: Which Should You Rent?

### Choose CB300R If:
- Your primary ride is Hai Van Pass
- You want agility + confidence in switchbacks
- You're lighter (<75 kg)
- Budget is primary concern
- You prefer minimalist bikes

### Choose Versys-X 300 If:
- You're doing 5+ day tours (comfort matters)
- Long highways (fairings block wind)
- You're taller (>180 cm; higher seat)
- You want adventure aesthetics + capability
- Budget is flexible

**Real-world verdict:** 80% of experienced riders choose CB300R for Hai Van Pass; 80% of long-tour riders choose Versys-X 300.
