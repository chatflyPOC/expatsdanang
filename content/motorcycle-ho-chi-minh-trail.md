# Ho Chi Minh Trail Big Bike Adventure: Da Nang to Hanoi Route Guide

**Published:** September 2026 | **Read time:** 5 minutes

The legendary Ho Chi Minh Trail (AKA "Mandala Route") stretches 900km through central Vietnam's mountains. Best ridden Sept-Oct; 5-7 days recommended.

## Route Overview
- **Da Nang → Ha Tay (starting point):** 300km (1 day)
- **Ha Tay → Khe Sanh (Trail heart):** 250km (2 days, technical)
- **Khe Sanh → Hanoi:** 350km (2-3 days)
- **Total:** 900km; 5-7 riding days + rest stops

## Best Bike for Trail
**CB500F or Versys 650** (power for climbs; comfort for long days; luggage capacity for remote sections)

## Challenges
- **No gas stations for 80km stretches** (fill before entering remote sections)
- **Rough roads:** 40% unpaved/washboard (requires high-clearance adventure bike)
- **Weather:** Afternoon thunderstorms June-Sept; rain-soaked sections dangerous
- **Solitude:** Few services; guide or experienced partner recommended

## Essential Gear
- Waterproof luggage (not all equipment waterproof)
- First aid kit
- Tool kit (basic wrenches, tire repair)
- Portable phone charger
- Offline maps (Locus Maps or Gaia GPS; 4G unreliable)

## Rental Companies Offering Trail Bikes
- Tuan Motorbike (experienced trail guides available)
- Style Motorbikes (premium trail equipment rental)
- Riderly (one-way Da Nang→Hanoi drop-off)

## Cost Breakdown (5-Day Trip)
- Bike rental: 2.5M VND (~$110)
- Insurance: 250k VND (~$11)
- Fuel: 300k VND (~$13)
- Accommodation (4 nights, budget): 800k VND (~$36)
- Food & activities: 600k VND (~$27)
- **Total:** ~$197/person
