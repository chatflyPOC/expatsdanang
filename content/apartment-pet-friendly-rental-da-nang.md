# Pet-Friendly Apartment Rentals in Da Nang: Housing for Expats with Dogs & Cats

**Published:** September 2026 | **Read time:** 4 minutes

Finding pet-friendly housing is harder than you'd think in Vietnam. Here's how.

## Pet-Friendly Neighborhoods

**Most lenient:** My Khe (international expats, fewer restrictions), Ngu Hanh Son
**Challenging:** Hai Chau, Cam Le (local landlords often prohibit pets)

## Typical Pet Policies

| Landlord Type | Dogs Allowed | Cats Allowed | Pet Deposit | Notes |
|---------------|-------------|------------|----------|-------|
| International rental company | Yes | Yes | 1-2M VND | Professional; transparent |
| Local landlord (younger) | Sometimes | Usually | 500k-1M | Negotiate directly |
| Local landlord (older) | Rarely | Sometimes | N/A | Cultural resistance to dogs |

## Monthly Cost Additions

- **Pet deposit:** 1-2M VND (non-refundable in some cases; negotiate)
- **Monthly pet fee:** 200-500k VND
- **Total:** Add 300-700k VND/month to rent

## Pet Logistics in Da Nang

**Veterinarian:** Go Vet (My Khe), Petcare (An Thuong) — English-speaking
**Pet supplies:** Petshop chains in malls; some expat Facebook groups sell supplies
**Airlines:** Most allow pets in cargo for flights out of Da Nang; $400-800 fee

## Finding Pet-Friendly Rentals

✅ Facebook: "Da Nang Expats," "Pet Owners Da Nang"
✅ Direct messaging landlords: Ask about pets BEFORE viewing
✅ Airbnb filter: "Pet-friendly" tag available

## Sample Pet Clause

"Tenant may keep one dog/cat under 10kg. Pet deposit of 1M VND required (refundable upon lease termination if no damage). Monthly pet fee: 300k VND."
