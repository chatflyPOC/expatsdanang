# International Driving Permit (IDP) for Motorcycle Rentals in Vietnam: 2026 Guide

**Published:** September 2026 | **Read time:** 5 minutes

Vietnam officially accepts International Driving Permits (IDP) for motorcycle rentals, but many first-timers don't know which IDP format is valid, how to obtain it, or what happens if you're stopped without one. This guide covers everything.

## Do You Actually Need an IDP?

### Vietnam's Official Rule (2024-2026)

| Situation | IDP Required? | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Riding with valid home-country license | No* | *If license is in English with motorcycle class |
| Riding with non-English license (e.g., Thai, Chinese) | Yes | Must have English translation |
| License is expired or missing motorcycle class | Yes | IDP fills the gap |
| Stopped by police for documentation check | Always have it | Prevents misunderstandings |

**Bottom line:** You *can* ride with just a valid home-country license if:
1. License clearly shows motorcycle/motorbike endorsement
2. License text is in English (or has English translation)
3. License is currently valid (not expired)

But police checkpoints are common. An IDP eliminates arguments.

## IDP Formats: Which One Works?

### Format 1: AATA/FÉDÉRATION INTERNATIONALE DE L'AUTOMOBILE (FIA) IDP

**Issued by:** National automobile clubs (AAA in US, CAA in Canada, AA in UK)

**Validity in Vietnam:** ✅ Fully accepted

**How it works:**
- Booklet format; 8-16 pages (looks like small passport)
- English translations of your license on every page
- Valid for 1 year from issue date
- Cost: $15–30 (varies by country/club)

**Pros:**
- Most widely recognized internationally
- Accepted at 150+ countries
- Includes liability insurance info (useful if accident)

**Cons:**
- Takes 1–2 weeks to obtain by mail
- Must renew annually

### Format 2: UN Convention IDP

**Issued by:** Some countries (UK Post Office, Canadian provinces)

**Validity in Vietnam:** ✅ Accepted (less common but valid)

**How it works:**
- Plastic card format (looks like credit card)
- English translations included
- Valid for 3 years (longer than AATA)
- Cost: $10–20

**Pros:**
- Accepted in some SE Asian countries
- 3-year validity (no annual renewal)

**Cons:**
- Harder to find in US/Canada
- Less widely recognized

### Format 3: Online/Digital IDP

**Issued by:** Various online services

**Validity in Vietnam:** ⚠️ Check first with rental

**How it works:**
- Digital certificate (printed or on phone)
- Issued online within hours
- Cost: $20–60

**Pros:**
- Fast (instant)
- No mail wait

**Cons:**
- Police may not accept digital-only (always print backup)
- Rental companies prefer physical
- Some online vendors are sketchy (use official DMV/AAA only)

**Recommendation:** If ordering online, use only official government sources (AAA.com, your country's DMV website).

## How to Get an IDP (By Country)

### USA

**Step 1:** Visit AAA.com or local AAA office
- Need valid US driver's license
- In-person or by mail

**Step 2:** Fill out application + pay fee ($15–20)

**Step 3:** Receive IDP within 7–14 days (mail) or instantly (in-person)

**Validity:** 1 year

**When to get:** At least 1 week before trip

### Canada

**Step 1:** Visit CAA office (English-speaking provinces) or Service BC (BC residents)

**Step 2:** Bring valid provincial driver's license + passport

**Step 3:** Pay $12–20; receive IDP same day or within 5 business days

**Validity:** 1 year

### UK

**Step 1:** Visit Post Office or apply via AA.com

**Step 2:** Bring UK photocard license

**Step 3:** Pay £5–8; receive IDP within 1–2 weeks

**Validity:** 1 year (AATA) or 3 years (UN)

### Australia

**Step 1:** Contact NRMA or AAA; apply online

**Step 2:** Bring valid Australian license

**Step 3:** Pay AUD $30–40; receive within 1 week

**Validity:** 1 year

### Recommended vendors (US/Canada/UK/AUS)

- ✅ AAA.com (USA)
- ✅ CAA.ca (Canada)
- ✅ theAA.com (UK)
- ✅ NRMA.com.au (Australia)
- ⚠️ 1Doc.com, IDPOnline.com — skip (high failure rate; police don't recognize)

## What to Do If Your License is Non-English

### If Your License Has No English Text

**Problem:** Police may not accept it; rental company may refuse.

**Solution:**

**Option 1: Get official translation**
- Contact your country's embassy in Vietnam
- Request "official English translation of driver's license"
- Cost: $10–20; turnaround 3–5 business days
- Bring original license + translation together

**Option 2: Get an IDP** (covers translation requirement)
- IDP is accepted by rental + police
- Don't need separate translation

**Recommendation:** Get IDP before trip (covers everything; no ambiguity).

## Police Checkpoints: What to Have Ready

### What Police Want to See

If stopped for document check:

| Document | Requirement | Preparedness |
|----------|------------|--------------|
| Passport | Required (ID verification) | Keep on person or nearby lodging |
| Home driver's license | Required | Keep in motorcycle glove box |
| IDP (if applicable) | Highly recommended | Keep in glove box with license |
| Motorcycle rental contract | Required (proof of authorization to ride this bike) | Clip to windscreen or in pocket |
| Travel insurance card | Optional but helpful | Keeps in phone photo |

### What to Say If Asked About IDP

**Scenario:** Police stop you; ask for documents.

**What to do:**
1. Stay calm; smile
2. Show passport first (ID)
3. Show motorcycle license
4. Show IDP if you have it ("Yes, I have international permit")
5. Show rental contract

**If police ask why no IDP:**
- "My license is in English; I didn't know IDP was required" (honest; usually accepted)
- Police rarely fine foreigners on first check
- Worst case: fine is 50,000–200,000 VND (~$2–8); annoying but not expensive

**Real scenario:** Checked at Hai Van Pass checkpoint
- Officer asks for license + IDP
- You show English-language US license + IDP
- Officer looks satisfied; waves you through (30 seconds)

## Common IDP Mistakes

### Mistake #1: Expired IDP

**Problem:** IDP must be valid on your travel date. Expired IDP = same as no IDP.

**Prevention:** Check expiration date before trip. If expiring within 2 weeks, renew before traveling.

### Mistake #2: Bringing Only IDP (No License)

**Problem:** IDP supplements license; doesn't replace it. Both together are required.

**Prevention:** Keep home license + IDP together always.

### Mistake #3: IDP Doesn't Match Motorcycle Class

**Problem:** Your home license says "B" (car only) but IDP doesn't show motorcycle endorsement.

**Prevention:** Verify before getting IDP. You need motorcycle class (A1, A2, or A) in home country to get motorcycle IDP.

### Mistake #4: Lost IDP Mid-Trip

**Problem:** IDP lost in motorcycle crash or theft. Police now see no IDP.

**Prevention:** Take photos of IDP front + back. Email to yourself (digital backup).

## FAQ: IDP & Motorcycle Rentals

**Q: Can I rent a motorcycle without an IDP if I have a valid home license?**
A: Rental company will likely accept English-language license. Police might question it. IDP eliminates all ambiguity.

**Q: Do I need a separate IDP for motorcycle vs car?**
A: No. One IDP covers all vehicle classes (car, motorcycle, truck).

**Q: What if my home license doesn't have motorcycle endorsement?**
A: You can't legally rent a motorcycle (Vietnam requires proof you can ride). Get motorcycle training + license in home country first.

**Q: How long is an IDP valid?**
A: Typically 1–3 years depending on format. Check when you receive it.

**Q: What's the worst-case scenario if stopped without IDP?**
A: Fine of 50,000–200,000 VND (~$2–8); possible bike confiscation (rare). Unlikely for tourists on first offense.

**Q: Can I use an expired IDP?**
A: No. Police may fine you. Get a new one before trip if yours is expiring.
