# Big Bike Rental Safety Checklist: Essential Pre-Ride Inspection for Da Nang

**Published:** September 2026 | **Read time:** 5 minutes

## Pre-Ride Safety: The 20-Point Checklist

Before you leave the rental office, inspect your big bike. This prevents accidents and disputes.

### Tire & Brake Inspection

- [ ] **Tire pressure:** Check both tires (psi listed on bike frame or manual)
- [ ] **Tire tread:** Run fingernail across grooves; should be >3mm (Hai Van Pass needs grip)
- [ ] **Tire damage:** Look for cuts, bulges, embedded objects
- [ ] **Brake pads:** Lever near handlebar should have >3mm friction material
- [ ] **Brake fluid:** Should be translucent, not dark/cloudy
- [ ] **Brake feel:** Squeeze lever; should require moderate pressure (not spongy)

### Lights & Visibility

- [ ] **Headlight:** High beam, low beam, brightness check
- [ ] **Taillight:** Brightness; blink pattern (if it's a blinker)
- [ ] **Turn signals:** Front left/right, rear left/right flashing correctly
- [ ] **Horn:** Loud, functional
- [ ] **Mirrors:** Both present, adjustable, clean

### Fluid Levels & Engine

- [ ] **Oil level:** Check with dipstick (if accessible); should be full
- [ ] **Fuel:** Fill tank completely (establish baseline)
- [ ] **Coolant:** Check reservoir (if applicable); full level
- [ ] **Battery:** Check terminals for corrosion; no loose wires

### Frame & Chassis

- [ ] **Steering:** Handlebars move freely; not grinding/squeaky
- [ ] **Suspension:** Test compression (press down on handlebars); should rebound smoothly
- [ ] **Fasteners:** Look for loose bolts around frame, engine mounts
- [ ] **Chain:** Check tension (should be ~2cm play in middle); not rusted
- [ ] **Sprocket:** Look for worn/bent teeth

### Pre-Ride Road Test (5 minutes)

- [ ] **Cold start:** Engine fires instantly
- [ ] **Idle:** Smooth RPM, no stalling
- [ ] **Throttle response:** Smooth acceleration 0–4,000 rpm
- [ ] **Braking:** Both brakes responsive; no pulsing/fading
- [ ] **Shifting:** Gears engage smoothly (if manual)

## Photo Documentation

**Take photos of:**
1. **Full bike (both sides)** — Shows overall condition
2. **Damage close-ups** — Any scratches, dents, cracks (zoom in)
3. **Odometer reading** — Establish baseline mileage
4. **Fuel gauge full** — Prove you took it full
5. **Tire tread** — Protects you if they claim wear damage
6. **Rental contract** — Signed agreement with damage disclaimers

**Pro tip:** Email photos to yourself immediately (time-stamped proof).

## Common Defects & How to Handle

| Issue | What to Do | Don't Ignore |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| Soft brake lever | Request brake bleed before leaving | Can fail on Hai Van descent |
| Low tire pressure | Ask rental to inflate to spec | Mountain passes need grip |
| Worn brake pads | Request pad replacement | Hai Van Pass demands strong brakes |
| Clunky chain | Ask for chain tension adjustment | Can seize mid-ride |
| Sluggish throttle | Request carb cleaning or replacement bike | Dangerous on hills |
| Cracked handlebar grips | Minor; acceptable; document photo | Grip failure in rain is risky |

**Golden rule:** If you're not 100% confident in a bike's safety, reject it and ask for replacement. Rental companies would rather swap bikes than deal with injury liability.

## On-Road Safety Habits

### Gear Every Ride
- [ ] Full helmet (DOT or ECE certified; non-negotiable)
- [ ] Gloves (protects hands in crash)
- [ ] Closed-toe shoes (mandatory for most rentals)
- [ ] Long sleeves/pants (sun + crash protection)

### Pre-Departure (Every Time)
- [ ] Check mirrors are adjusted for your height
- [ ] Confirm fuel is ample for your route
- [ ] Have rental company emergency number saved
- [ ] Download offline maps (4G is spotty)
- [ ] Let someone know your route + expected arrival

### Riding Discipline

**On Hai Van Pass:**
- Brake before the turn, not mid-curve
- Downshift to engine-brake descents (brakes stay cooler)
- Never overtake on blind curves
- Watch for loose gravel on outer edges
- Respect the road's 13 switchbacks

**On Highway 1:**
- Match traffic pace (60–80 km/h typical)
- Stay right; watch for left-turning vehicles
- Watch for cyclists/scooters on road edges
- Avoid riding after dark
- Stay alert at villages (children, animals crossing)

## Accident Protocol

If you crash or damage the bike:

1. **Don't move the bike** (unless blocking traffic dangerously)
2. **Call rental company immediately** (emergency number on contract)
3. **Get police report** (Công an; they'll document damage)
4. **Take photos** of damage + accident scene
5. **Get witness contacts** (if other vehicle involved)
6. **Seek medical care** if injured (don't downplay injuries)
7. **Review insurance claim** with rental company (they should cover)

**Cost reality:** Minor crash = $200–500 damage (covered by insurance if you paid for it). Major crash = $2,000–5,000+ (you may be liable).

## Common Safety Mistakes (Avoid These)

| Mistake | Consequence | Prevention |
|---------|------------|-----------|
| Riding without helmet | Police fine + serious injury | Wear helmet every single ride |
| Wet-weather overconfidence | Loss of grip on curves | Reduce speed 20% when wet |
| Fatigue riding | Microsleep; crash | Rest after 2 hours riding |
| Unfamiliar bike | Poor brake/throttle feel | Take 10 min local ride after pickup |
| Fuel reserve too low | Stranded without gas stations | Fill before mountain passes |
| Phone navigation distraction | Swerving, crashes | Use phone holders; glance briefly |
