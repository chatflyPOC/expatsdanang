## Safety Rules

1. **Helmets mandatory** (plus goggles for dust)
2. **Long pants/gloves** (required by rental)
3. **Ride within skill level** (many riders overestimate)
4. **Insurance essential** ($15-25/day; off-road damage likely)

## Rental Requirements

- Motorcycle license (home country or IDP)
- Age 18+; experience preferred
- $300-500 damage deposit
- Comprehensive insurance (non-negotiable for off-road)
