# Understanding Lease Agreements in Vietnam: Legal Guide for Expats

**Published:** September 2026 | **Read time:** 5 minutes

Vietnam lease contracts are often landlord-favorable. Know your rights.

## What Vietnamese Leases Require

1. **Tenant identification:** Passport + visa copy
2. **Guarantor:** Sometimes required; can be rental company
3. **Terms:** Rent, term length, late penalties (typically 50k–100k VND/day)
4. **Deposit clause:** Usually equal to 1 month's rent

## Red Flags in Contracts

🚨 "Landlord can enter anytime" — Negotiable; agree on 24-hour notice
🚨 "No guests after 10pm" — Unenforceable; negotiate out
🚨 "Early termination = 2 months penalty" — Try to limit to 1 month
🚨 "Deposit non-refundable" — Resist this; deposits should be refundable

## Temporary Residence Registration (TRR)

**Mandatory:** Police filing required within 24 hours of arrival.
**What you need:** Passport, lease, landlord signature
**Cost:** Free (landlord usually handles)
**Duration:** Valid until lease ends

## Contract Language

Request **English translation** if your Vietnamese is weak. Verbal agreements are worthless.

## Sample Clause Template

"The deposit of [amount] will be refunded within 7 days of lease termination, less any damages documented with photos by both parties."
