# How to Avoid Big Bike Rental Scams: Da Nang 2026 Safety Guide

**Published:** September 2026 | **Read time:** 5 minutes

Real scams reported by renters; how to spot and avoid them.

## Scam 1: Inflated Damage Claims
**Red flag:** You return bike with minor scratch; rental claims $2,000 damage.
**Protection:** Photo + written pre-ride inspection; refuse to sign damage forms without photos attached.

## Scam 2: "Lost" Rental Agreement
**Red flag:** Rental keeps original; claims you owe extra fees (no proof).
**Protection:** Request 2 copies signed before pickup; keep one in backpack.

## Scam 3: Insurance Not Actually Included
**Red flag:** "Insurance included" in quotes, but fine print says $500 deductible.
**Protection:** Ask in writing what's covered; get contact info for insurance company directly.

## Scam 4: Bait-and-Switch Bike
**Red flag:** "Your CB300R isn't ready; here's a Harley instead" (bike you didn't book).
**Protection:** Reserve by VIN/specific unit; take photos of registration; refuse substitutes.

## Scam 5: Hidden Charges at Return
**Red flag:** "Gas tank not full; $50." or "Chain dirty; $30 cleaning fee."
**Protection:** Fill tank before return; document fuel gauge photo; clarify all fees in writing before departure.
