## Best One-Way Routes for Big Bikes

### Da Nang → Hanoi (Northern Explorer)
- **Day 1:** Da Nang → Hai Phong (320 km; 6 hours via Highway 1)
- **Day 2:** Hai Phong → Hanoi (150 km; 3 hours)
- **Highlights:** Ha Long Bay detour, Hanoi Old Quarter
- **Bike:** 500cc+ recommended (long distances; highway riding)

### Da Nang → Saigon (Southern Crosser)
- **Day 1:** Da Nang → Nha Trang (280 km; 6 hours)
- **Day 2:** Nha Trang → Ho Chi Minh City (620 km; split into 2 days)
- **Highlights:** Nha Trang beaches, coastal riding
- **Bike:** 400cc+ recommended (long riding days)

### Ho Chi Minh City → Da Nang (Northbound Explorer)
- **Reverse:** Same routes but northbound; scenic both directions
- **Popular combo:** HCMC → Da Nang → Hanoi (10–14 days)

## One-Way Rental Logistics

**Pickup:** Da Nang office (or your hotel for +50k fee)
**Dropoff:** Partner rental office in destination city
**Fuel:** Return with full tank (or pay 30% markup)
**Deposit:** Same $200–500 hold; released after inspection

---

## Highway Riding Tips for Long Distances

1. **Break every 2 hours** (leg rest, fuel check)
2. **Ride early mornings** (cooler; avoid afternoon traffic)
3. **Stay hydrated** (Vietnamese highways are hot; drink 2L water/day)
4. **Night riding:** Avoid—roads less safe; poor lighting in villages
5. **Rest days:** Budget 1 rest day per 1,000 km (Nha Trang, Hai Phong)

---

## Bike Recommendations for One-Way

- **Up to 5 days:** 300–400cc (comfort, fuel economy)
- **5–10 days:** 500cc+ (power, highway stability)
- **10+ days:** 650cc+ (comfort, luggage capacity)

## FAQ: One-Way Rentals

**Q: Can I rent one-way on a 300cc bike?**
A: Yes, but recommend upgrading to 400cc+ for highway endurance.

**Q: What if the bike breaks down on the highway?**
A: Rental company covers towing + repair; you're not liable if it's not your fault.

**Q: Can I do Da Nang → Hanoi in 2 days?**
A: Technically yes (1,000 km; push it to 500 km/day) but dangerous. Recommend 3–4 days minimum.
