# Best Neighborhoods for Expats in Da Nang: Complete Guide to Districts, Costs & Lifestyle

**Published:** September 2026 | **Read time:** 7 minutes

Da Nang has five main neighborhoods for expats. Here's where to live based on lifestyle, budget, and community.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **My Khe** | Beach resort; touristy | 8–12M VND | High (70%) | Tourists, digital nomads, young expats |

## My Khe Beach (Hai Chau District)

**Vibe:** International resort area; touristy; party scene

**Who lives here:** Digital nomads, vacation-mode expats, young foreigners (20s–30s)

**Geography:**
- Runs along 30km of white sand beach
- Main drag: Vo Nguyen Giap Street (restaurants, bars, shops)
- Walking distance: Bakeries, coffee shops, beach access

**Cost of living:**
- 1BR apartment: $350–500/month
- Dining out: $8–15 (touristy inflated)
- Coffee: $3–5
- Gym membership: $30–50/month

**Expat infrastructure:**
- ✅ English widely spoken
- ✅ Nightlife (bars, clubs)
- ✅ International restaurants
- ✅ Tourist services everywhere
- ✅ Easy to find English-speaking locals

**Cons:**
- Very expensive (2x neighborhood prices)
- Noisy (tourist area; beach parties)
- Few local Vietnamese (less cultural immersion)
- Cramped (tourist season crowded)

**Best for:** Digital nomads, short-term expats, party seekers

**Schools:** None major (too touristy for families)

**Rent negotiation:** Hard (landlords know tourists pay premium; prices fixed)

---

## An Thuong (Hai Chau District)

**Vibe:** Mixed local-expat; vibrant; real neighborhood feel

**Who lives here:** Families, established expats, professionals

**Geography:**
- South of My Khe (2km inland)
- Main commercial street: Phan Boi Chau
- Residential streets: Quiet; tree-lined
- Close to schools + services

**Cost of living:**
- 1BR apartment: $250–350/month
- Dining out: $4–8 (mix tourist + local)
- Coffee: $1.50–3
- Gym membership: $20–35/month

**Expat infrastructure:**
- ✅ Good English (mixed community)
- ✅ International schools nearby (ABC International, DNIS)
- ✅ Family-friendly restaurants
- ✅ Supermarkets (Co.opmart, Saigon Co.op)
- ✅ Medical clinics (FVHospital, Raffles)

**Cons:**
- Less nightlife than My Khe
- Mixed neighborhood (less "expat bubble")
- Construction noise occasionally (development area)

**Best for:** Families, long-term residents, professionals

**Schools:** ABC International School (1km away), DNIS (3km away)

**Rent negotiation:** Medium (some vacant units; negotiate 5–10%)

**Sample rent: $300/month, negotiate to $270 for 12-month lease**

## Ngu Hanh Son (Ngu Hanh Son District)

**Vibe:** Expat village; peaceful; mountain views

**Who lives here:** Families, retirees, long-term expats (3+ years)

**Geography:**
- Southeast of An Thuong (across Han River)
- Marble Mountains nearby (scenic)
- Quiet residential; less traffic
- Overlooking beach from distance

**Cost of living:**
- 1BR apartment: $300–400/month
- Dining out: $5–10 (mostly expat restaurants)
- Coffee: $2–4
- Gym membership: $25–40/month

**Expat infrastructure:**
- ✅ Highest expat concentration (feel at home)
- ✅ English common (75%+ expats)
- ✅ Expat social groups active (book clubs, sports, expat meetups)
- ✅ Good international restaurants
- ✅ Peaceful; safe
- ✅ Near ABC International School (main campus)

**Cons:**
- Less local culture (expat bubble)
- Fewer Vietnamese restaurants
- Quieter nightlife
- Slightly more isolated (12km from city center)

**Best for:** Long-term expats, families, anyone seeking community

**Schools:** ABC International School (main campus; 5km away)

**Rent negotiation:** Medium (expat landlords; some flexibility)

**Sample rent: $350/month, negotiate to $320 for 12 months**

## Hai Chau District (Local Vietnamese Neighborhoods)

**Vibe:** Authentic Vietnamese; local; budget-friendly

**Who lives here:** Budget expats, Vietnamese speakers, cultural learners

**Geography:**
- Central Da Nang (walking distance to everything)
- Mixed residential-commercial
- Less manicured than expat areas

**Cost of living:**
- 1BR apartment: $180–280/month
- Dining out: $2–4 (street food, banh mi)
- Coffee: $0.50–$1.50
- Gym membership: $10–20/month

**Expat infrastructure:**
- ❌ Limited English
- ❌ Fewer international services
- ✅ Authentic neighborhood
- ✅ Real cultural experience
- ✅ Cheapest cost of living
- ✅ Good public transport

**Cons:**
- Language barrier significant
- No international schools
- Limited healthcare options (FVH is 20 min away)
- Noisy (local traffic, motorbikes)

**Best for:** Language learners, budget travelers, solo long-term expats

**Schools:** None (not expat family area)

**Rent negotiation:** Easy (lower demand; negotiate 10–15%)

---

## Linh Dang (Suburban)

**Vibe:** Quiet suburb; residential; peaceful

**Who lives here:** Remote workers, quiet seekers, budget expats

**Geography:**
- North of central Da Nang (20 min drive to My Khe)
- Suburban feel; green spaces
- Residential buildings + family homes
- Quieter; away from tourist areas

**Cost of living:**
- 1BR apartment: $150–250/month
- Dining out: $3–6 (local Vietnamese)
- Coffee: $1–2
- Gym membership: $15–25/month

**Expat infrastructure:**
- ❌ Very few expats
- ❌ Limited English
- ✅ Peaceful
- ✅ Cheapest
- ✅ Real Vietnamese community
- ⚠️ Longer commute to beaches/city center

**Cons:**
- Isolated (no walkable neighborhood)
- Few restaurants/cafes
- No international schools
- Requires motorbike or taxi for everything

**Best for:** Remote workers seeking quiet, budget expats, those not needing community

**Schools:** None

**Rent negotiation:** Very easy (low demand; 10–20% discount possible)

## Neighborhood Recommendation by Situation

### "I'm just arriving; want to meet other expats"
→ **My Khe or Ngu Hanh Son**
- My Khe: Immediate social life; meet travelers
- Ngu Hanh Son: Meet long-term expats; slower but deeper connections

### "I'm moving with a family; need schools"
→ **An Thuong or Ngu Hanh Son**
- ABC International or DNIS nearby
- Family-friendly restaurants
- Parks + community

### "I want the best value for money"
→ **Hai Chau or Linh Dang**
- $5–8 rent = $150–280/month
- Lowest cost of living
- Trade-off: Less English; fewer services

### "I'm remote worker; want peace + low cost"
→ **Linh Dang or suburban Hai Chau**
- Quiet neighborhood
- $200/month rent leaves budget for co-working
- Worth 20-min commute for peace

### "I want balance: expat amenities + local culture"
→ **An Thuong (best choice)**
- Mixed expat-local neighborhood
- Good schools; good restaurants
- $300/month = best value in this range
- Rent negotiable

## FAQ

**Q: Which neighborhood is safest?**
A: All are safe. Ngu Hanh Son feels safest (most policing); Hai Chau requires street smarts (pickpocketing in crowded areas).

**Q: Can I rent for a month?**
A: My Khe: Yes (tourists). Other areas: Usually 6-month minimum; negotiate.

**Q: Which has best food?**
A: Hai Chau (authentic; cheapest). My Khe (international; expensive). An Thuong (both).

**Q: Do I need a motorbike?**
A: My Khe/An Thuong: No (walkable + buses). Ngu Hanh Son: Recommended (15+ min walks). Linh Dang: Required.

**Q: Best for digital nomads?**
A: My Khe (social; WiFi) or An Thuong (balance). Avoid Linh Dang (too quiet; long commute).
